Key summary: the images, GIFs and photos you select are processed only inside your own browser, and we never receive, store or view those files or their contents. The only thing sent to the server is the ordinary access log produced when you open this page, plus advertising-related requests.

This Privacy Policy applies to every feature and service offered by the Sprite Sheet Splitter (the "Service"). 3. How the images and GIFs you select are handled

The core principle of this Service is that there is no file upload. Processing works as follows.

  1. When you choose an image or GIF in your browser's file selection dialog, the file data is delivered only to the memory of the device on which your browser is running.
  2. All processing — splitting, cutting, background removal, ZIP compression — runs inside the browser. Our server plays no part in it, and the processed results are not sent anywhere either.
  3. Result files are saved to a location you choose through your browser's download function. We cannot check whether the saved files exist or what they contain.

Even if an image contains sensitive information or material protected by copyright, we cannot view or store its contents. This is exactly why the answer to "Do you upload images to a server?" in our frequently asked questions is "no".

4. Use of cookies and web browser storage features

The Service sets no cookies of its own for convenience. It also uses no third-party cookies for advertising. Browser storage features (local storage and the like) are not used either.

6. Measures to secure personal information

  • The website is served only over HTTPS (TLS), so data is encrypted in transit.
  • Your image data is processed only in browser memory and is never recorded in server logs or stored.
  • Email addresses collected while responding to an enquiry are used only to send the reply, and for no other purpose.
  • We have established a separate procedure for receiving your objections so that any infringement of your rights is minimised.

7. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may demand access to, correction of, deletion of, or a stop to the processing of your personal information at any time. You can make such a demand through the method below, and we will handle it without undue delay after verification, to the extent possible.

  • Method of making a demand: send an email to the address listed on the Contact page
  • Required details: your name, a reachable email address and the specific request you are making
  • Processing time: handled without undue delay after receipt, and the outcome is reported back to you by reply

8. Personal information of children under 14

The Service is not a service that children under the age of 14 may use. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children under 14. If a minor needs to use the Service, please be sure to obtain the consent of a parent or legal guardian.
